Steak Recipe

Description

A classic steak is a cut of beef that is grilled or pan-seared to perfection. It's a simple dish that highlights the quality of the meat and the skill of the cook.

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeye steaks (about 1 inch thick)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 2 sprigs of fresh rosemary (optional)

Steps

  1. Take the steaks out of the refrigerator and let them come to room temperature for about 30 minutes.
  2. Season both sides of the steaks generously with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat a cast-iron skillet or grill pan over high heat until it's very hot.
  4. Add the olive oil to the pan and swirl to coat.
  5. Place the steaks in the pan and sear for 3-4 minutes on each side for medium-rare, or longer if you prefer your steak more done.
  6. Add the minced garlic and rosemary to the pan during the last minute of cooking for added flavor.
  7. Remove the steaks from the pan and let them rest for 5 minutes before slicing and serving.
